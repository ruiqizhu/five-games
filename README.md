# Five Games

Five small games that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to install. Best scores are saved in local storage on your device.

**Play:** https://ruiqizhu.github.io/five-games/

## Snake Rush

A classic snake that speeds up as it grows. Apples are worth 10 points, stars are a +50 bonus, and hourglasses trigger a short slow-mo.

- Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, or a swipe.
- Space or Enter starts a run. After a game over, R, Space, or Enter restarts.
- P pauses.
- M toggles sound.

## Brick Breaker

Smash colorful bricks across 6 levels with a glowing paddle and 3 lives.

- Move the paddle with the mouse, a finger, the left and right arrow keys, or A and D.
- Click, tap, or press Space to launch the ball.
- Space or the Play button starts a game. R restarts when the round is over.
- M toggles sound.

## Space Dodger

Weave a ship through an asteroid field that keeps getting faster. The score is how long you survive.

- Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, or the mouse or finger.
- Space or Launch starts a run. R or Space restarts after a crash.
- M toggles sound.

## Memory Match

Flip emoji cards and find all 8 pairs. The best score is the fewest moves.

- Click or tap a card to flip it.
- R or Restart deals a new board.
- M toggles sound.

## Flappy Blob

Flap a squishy blob through the gaps between pipes.

- Click, Space, the up arrow, W, or a tap flaps.
- After a crash, click, Space, or R restarts.
- M toggles sound.
