# Five Games

Five small games that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to install. Best scores are saved in local storage on your device.

**Play:** https://ruiqizhu.github.io/five-games/

## Snake Rush

A classic snake that speeds up as it grows. Every 5 fruits raises the level. From level 2 on, short walls are added to the board. Apples are worth 10. Bonus pickups are a star (+50), slow-mo, ghost (pass through your own body), magnet (food steps toward you), and shrink (cuts your length). Active power-ups show a countdown in the bar above the board.

- Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, or a swipe.
- Space or Enter starts a run. After a game over, R, Space, or Enter restarts.
- P pauses.
- M toggles sound.

## Brick Breaker

Clear bricks through 9 levels with a glowing paddle and 3 lives. Ball speed steps up a little each level. Some bricks are unbreakable, some explode and clear their neighbors, and every 3rd level is a moving boss. Destroyed bricks sometimes drop multiball, a wider paddle, a laser, or a sticky paddle. Power-up timers sit in the top bar.

- Move the paddle with the mouse, a finger, the left and right arrow keys, or A and D.
- Click, tap, or press Space to launch the ball. While a ball is stuck to a sticky paddle, that same button launches it again.
- When the laser is active and the ball is already in play, click, tap, or Space fires.
- Space or the Play button starts a game. R restarts when the round is over.
- M toggles sound.

## Space Dodger

Weave a ship through an asteroid field that keeps getting faster. Your saved score is still how long you survive. Coins add a bonus point total for the run. A shield absorbs one hit. A laser pickup fires for a few seconds (it also pulses on its own). Cracked asteroids break into two smaller rocks when shot. Every so often a wave of homing mines joins the field.

- Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, or the mouse or finger.
- Space or Launch starts a run. During a run, Space fires while the laser is active. R or Space restarts after a crash.
- M toggles sound.

## Memory Match

Flip emoji cards and find every pair. Choose Easy (4×3, 6 pairs), Medium (4×4, 8 pairs), or Hard (6×4, 12 pairs), and a deck of animals, food, or space. Solo play saves the fewest moves for that grid size (the original medium record is kept). Two-player mode is local and turn-based: a match scores that player a pair and they go again. The winner screen shows both scores.

- Click or tap a card to flip it. The grid, deck, and solo / 2-player buttons sit above the board.
- R or Restart deals a new board with the current settings.
- M toggles sound.

## Flappy Blob

Flap a squishy blob through gaps between pipes. The saved score is pipes passed plus coins collected. After 5 pipes, new gaps weave up and down. Coins sit in some gaps. A shield pickup absorbs one hit. The sky, hills, and ground shift as your pipe count climbs.

- Click, Space, the up arrow, W, or a tap flaps.
- After a crash, click, Space, or R restarts.
- M toggles sound.
